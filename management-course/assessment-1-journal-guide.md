# Assessment 1 写作指南：Tutorial Workbook & Self-Reflective Journals

> 本指南帮助你理解作业要求和评分标准，并提供结构模板。日志内容必须基于**你自己**在 tutorial 中的经历、问卷结果和小组讨论来写——这正是评分的核心（personalisation、self-assessment）。

## 1. 作业要求速览

| 项目 | 要求 |
| --- | --- |
| 数量 | 4 篇日志，从 Week 5 起 |
| 权重 | 40%（每篇按 100 分评分，合计折算为 40 分） |
| 截止 | 21 Dec 23:59，并提交到 Canvas Assignment Dropbox |
| 格式 | 每篇另起一页，标明 **tutorial 名称和日期** |
| 每篇结构 | (1) Tutorial Summary：2–3 段，300 词 ±5%（285–315 词）<br>(2) Self-Reflection：2–3 段，300 词 ±5%（285–315 词） |
| 每篇总字数 | 约 600 词 |
| 迟交 | 每天扣 10%；超过 5 个自然日不予评分（除非获批延期） |

## 2. 评分标准拆解（每篇 100 分）

| 标准 | 分值 | HD 的要求 | 你需要做到 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Essential Knowledge of Business & Management Skills Concepts | 40 | 深入反思并**个人化**课程中的理论、概念、策略；超出所有要求 | 每篇明确点名 1–2 个课程理论，正确解释，并联系到自己 |
| Communication | 20 | 清晰、简洁、组织良好；**没有**拼写、语法、句法错误 | 每段一个中心思想；用连接词；提交前逐句校对 |
| Critical Analysis | 40 | 综合整个学期的观点；详细说明这些洞见对自己学习的影响；学习进程的演变"确凿" | 把本周内容和之前的周次、自测结果联系起来；写出具体的改进计划；体现"我的想法变了" |

**最容易丢分的地方：**

- 只描述活动，没有理论 → Knowledge 失分
- 理论讲得很好，但没有联系自己 → 缺少 personalisation
- 每篇各自孤立，看不出成长 → Critical Analysis 只能拿到 C
- 字数超出或不足 ±5% → 不满足 instructions
- 语法小错误 → HD 要求"no errors"

## 3. 单篇日志模板

```
Journal [1–4]: [Tutorial name]
Date: [dd/mm/yyyy]

Part 1 – Tutorial Summary (285–315 words)

Paragraph 1 – What happened
  - The topic and the theory/model it covered (name it).
  - The activities: questionnaire, group task, case study, game.

Paragraph 2 – Results and answers
  - Your questionnaire score and what it means (e.g. "I scored 4.2/5 on ...").
  - Your group's answers to the case study questions, summarised.
  - Your own position in the discussion.

(Paragraph 3 – optional) Key takeaway the tutor or group agreed on.

Part 2 – Self-Reflection (285–315 words)

Paragraph 1 – Reaction and insight (What? So what?)
  - How you felt about the activity, and what surprised you.
  - What it revealed about you, linked to the theory.

Paragraph 2 – Connection and critical analysis
  - Link to a real experience (work, part-time job, group assignment, society).
  - Link to an earlier tutorial or self-assessment: does it confirm or challenge it?
  - Where the theory has limits for you (culture, context).

Paragraph 3 – Improvement plan (Now what?)
  - One or two specific, measurable actions with a timeframe.
  - How you will know you have improved.
```

## 4. 反思框架：What? → So what? → Now what?（Rolfe 等, 2001）

| 阶段 | 问自己 | 对应评分标准 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **What?** | 发生了什么？我做了什么？结果是什么？ | Summary 部分 |
| **So what?** | 这说明了我的什么？和理论有什么关系？和之前的经历/周次有什么联系？ | Knowledge + Critical Analysis |
| **Now what?** | 我接下来会怎么做？如何检验？ | Critical Analysis（implications for learning） |

也可以使用 Gibbs 反思循环（Description → Feelings → Evaluation → Analysis → Conclusion → Action plan）。

## 5. 让 Critical Analysis 拿高分的技巧

1. **建立贯穿线**：在第一篇日志就点出你最想提升的 1–2 项管理技能（比如"给负面反馈"），后续每篇都回到这条线上。
2. **前后对照**：用这样的句式——"In Week 5 I believed …; after this week's activity, I now realise …"
3. **用数据说话**：引用你的问卷分数，并与班级平均水平或自己之前的分数比较。
4. **批判理论本身**：理论在什么情况下不适用？（例如：在高权力距离的文化中，直接的反馈方式未必有效。）
5. **最后一篇做总结**：用一段话回顾 4 周以来的变化，以及对未来学习和职业的影响——这直接对应 "evolution in learning process is conclusive"。

## 6. 示范段落（仅展示写法，请勿照抄）

下面是一个虚构主题 "Giving Feedback" 的 Self-Reflection 段落，用来展示**理论 + 个人化 + 前后联系 + 行动计划**的写法：

> Completing the feedback questionnaire was uncomfortable because my score of 2.8 out of 5 on "delivering negative feedback" confirmed a pattern I had previously ignored. During a group assignment last semester, I rewrote a teammate's section myself rather than telling him it lacked evidence. At the time I believed I was protecting the group's harmony; however, the SBI model discussed in class made me realise that avoiding the conversation denied him the chance to improve and increased my own workload. This also connects to the Week 5 self-assessment, where I identified myself as conflict-avoidant. Over the next four weeks, I will use the SBI structure at least once in each group meeting and ask a peer to tell me whether my feedback was specific and respectful.

注意这段话：点名了理论（SBI），引用了问卷分数，联系了真实经历和之前的周次，并给出了可检验的行动。

## 7. 常用学术表达

| 用途 | 表达 |
| --- | --- |
| 引入理论 | According to …, / The … model suggests that … |
| 联系自己 | This resonated with my experience of … / This made me realise that … |
| 转折 / 批判 | However, … / Although the model is useful, it assumes that … |
| 前后对照 | Compared with my earlier belief that …, I now … |
| 行动计划 | Moving forward, I intend to … / To develop this skill, I will … |
